We're part of a generation that got to experience a little bit of everything. We played with cassette tapes and learned how to rewind them manually. We were outdoor kids as much as we were indoor kids. We got beaten up by our moms (sometimes deserved, sometimes not 😂), and somehow, we still ended up having one of the greatest childhoods.

We also got to witness the rise of smartphones and experience some of the greatest cars and bikes ever made, some of which are still better than what we see rolling out today.

02

And then we had to survive the end of the world (or at least what felt like it), wars, bombs, COVID-19, and now one of the worst economic crises and periods of inflation Sri Lanka has ever seen. Yet, here we are, somehow doing okay.

I think a part of that comes down to the decisions we've made along the way. Sure, some of them were bad, and some might still haunt us (maybe not), but every single one of those decisions played a part in shaping who we are today. And I guess that's what matters. Along the way, you also found something (or someone, in this case, a certain little troublemaker 🐈) that means a lot to you, even if it comes with its fair share of headaches.
